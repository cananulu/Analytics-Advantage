Key Managerial Insights

1. Customer Retention

The repeat purchase rate is 78.8%. Focus retention campaigns on the 21% of one-time buyers — targeted follow-up mailings within 30 days of first purchase can lift repeat rates significantly.

2. Loyalty Program ROI

Only 35.6% of customers are loyalty members. Compare their lifetime value to non-members in the chart above — if members are materially more valuable, investing in enrollment incentives will improve margins.

3. Channel Effectiveness

Use the "Avg LTV by Acquisition Channel" chart to identify which channel brings the most valuable customers, not just the most customers. Shift marketing spend toward channels with higher LTV.

4. Product Mix Optimization

Compare the product category breakdown (volume) with average order value per category. Categories with high AOV but low volume may benefit from increased catalog placement.

5. Regional Strategy

Cross-reference the revenue-by-region chart with customer counts. Under-penetrated regions with strong per-customer revenue are prime candidates for geographic expansion.

6. Age-Based Targeting

Examine the average orders by age group chart. If older segments order more frequently, direct mail — which skews older in readership — is well-positioned. Digital channels may be needed to grow the younger segments.
